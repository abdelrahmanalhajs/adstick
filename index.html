Platform Features
Everything you need to run car ad campaigns

A complete toolkit for advertisers, fleet managers, and campaign analysts.

📍

Real-time GPS Tracking

See every car with your sticker on a live map. Updates every 30 seconds with precise coordinates.

📏

KM Distance Analytics

Track total kilometers each car has traveled with your sticker on it. Broken down by day, week, or campaign.

🌐

Traffic Density Maps

Visualize how much traffic exists in areas where your cars are operating — maximize exposure.

Campaign Dashboard

Manage multiple ad campaigns, assign cars to campaigns, set durations, and track progress.

Drivers can log in to see their routes, earnings, and sticker status from a dedicated interface.

📈

Client Reports

Auto-generate PDF reports for your advertising clients with reach estimates and coverage maps.

How it Works
From sticker to insights in 4 steps
1

Add Campaign

Create a campaign with brand, duration, target areas, and KM goals.

2

Assign Cars

Select cars from the fleet and assign them to the campaign. Stickers are placed physically.

3

Cars Drive

GPS devices track location, speed, and routes in real time as cars move through the city.

4

Track & Report

View live map, KM analytics, traffic exposure, and share reports with clients.
